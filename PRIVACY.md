# Revisit privacy notice (beta draft)

Revisit stores content only when a user explicitly clicks **Research** or **Capture**. A capture can include selected text, the page URL and title, site metadata, an optional note, and the capture time.

Research captures may be sent to configured AI, embedding, web-search, extraction, and observability providers to create research results. Capture-only items are stored but are not selected for the research pipeline. Revisit does not sell captured content.

Users can request an export or permanent account deletion through the beta support contact. Deletion removes the account and associated active data; encrypted backups expire according to the hosting provider's retention period. The production policy must list the operator contact, subprocessors, retention periods, and effective date before public release.
